Intervals quantify training-seed uncertainty conditional on these fixed partitions. They do not capture uncertainty over datasets, domain sampling or deployment traffic; five seeds give limited precision.

Operating-point protocol: baseline threshold 0.5 plus validation FPR caps 0.1%, 0.5% and 1%. For each cap, select the ROC threshold with maximum validation recall among feasible thresholds (ties favor the first/higher threshold). Freeze it and evaluate on test data. No threshold is selected from test scores. Test FPR need not meet its validation cap. Validation contains 4,055 benign URLs, limiting low-FPR resolution.

Precision projections at 0.1% and 1% malicious prevalence are hypothetical prior-shift scenarios, not measured Ghana traffic rates. They assume test TPR/FPR transfer to deployment. The positive label includes phishing, malware and defacement; these are malicious-prevalence projections, not phishing-only estimates. PR curves and average precision reflect the observed test prevalence (~47%).
